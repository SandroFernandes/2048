How to play

  1. Slide. Swipe on the board, or press the arrow keys or W A S D. Every tile slides as far as it can in that direction.
  2. Merge. Two tiles with the same number combine into one tile with their sum. A tile can merge only once per move, so a row of 2 2 2 2 slid left becomes 4 4.
  3. Grow. After every move that changes the board, a new 2 (or occasionally a 4) appears in an empty cell.
  4. Score. Each merge adds the value of the new tile to your score.
  5. Win. Create the target tile (1024, 2048 or 4096). You can then keep going in Endless Mode.
  6. Game over. The game ends when the board is full and no neighbouring tiles match. You can undo your last moves.
